Recovery Kit

Made · Backups go to · Kit ID

    These words are the only key to your backups. Mirrin doesn't keep them, so nobody can give them back if they're lost. Anyone who has them can read your backups.

    Write them on paper and keep it somewhere safe and offline. Not in a photo or a note on your phone. They're shown once: this page can't show them again.

    Bringing back

    1. On the new computer (or this one after a reinstall), install Mirrin and open it.
    2. In the Welcome window, choose I already have a twin, pick where your backups are, and type your 12 words. Or type mirrin restore in Terminal, which does the same.
    3. Then check the devices that can reach it: a backup from before you removed a device would bring it back.
